Questions

Before you call.

Anything not covered here, ask us on WhatsApp.

Do you do the work yourselves?

Work is carried out by vetted providers who work to our checklists. You deal with us for scheduling, quality and follow-up, so if something goes wrong you call one number and we sort it out.

What does a Property Assessment involve?

We walk your premises with you, note what needs regular attention and send a written summary with a recommended schedule and a quote. The assessment fee is credited in full if you start a Care Plan within 30 days.

How is a Care Plan priced?

By site, frequency and scope. The quote lists each service, how often it happens, the visit window, and what is and isn't included. Prices are valid for 30 days and invoiced monthly in advance.

Which areas do you cover?

Bulawayo and Harare. If your property is elsewhere, message us and we'll tell you honestly whether we can serve it well.

Can you handle plumbing and electrical repairs?

We coordinate repairs through qualified providers. If we don't have the right specialist for a job, we'll tell you rather than send someone who isn't.

What happens if I'm not happy with a visit?

Message us within 48 hours of the visit, in the same WhatsApp thread. We'll review the record and photos with you and put it right.

Do you clean private homes?

We focus on commercial properties: offices, shared buildings, guesthouses, restaurants and shops. That focus is how we keep the standard consistent.
